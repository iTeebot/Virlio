# Community Standards & Trust Moderation Guidelines

## Executive Overview
Virlio maintains a high-quality, brand-safe ecosystem for verified businesses and creators.

## Content Safety & Moderation Pipeline
1. **Automated Vision & OCR Filter**: Scans uploaded templates and generated memes for explicit content, hate speech, and copyright infringement.
2. **Duplicate Detection Scoring**: Perceptual hashing (pHash) detects duplicate template submissions.
3. **Community Reporting Escalation**:
   - 1 Report: Queued for moderation review.
   - 5 Reports: Hidden automatically from public feeds pending staff audit.
   - Verified Brand Exemption: Priority human audit queue for brand assets.
4. **Author Trust Score**: Accounts with 95%+ approved templates receive instant publishing privileges.
